Save to Home Screen

Add yachtspotter to your device for quick access with the custom anchor icon:

For iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button in the browser toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add in the top right corner.

For iPad / iPhone (Chrome):

  1. Tap the Share icon in the top right address bar.
  2. Select "Add to Home Screen" from the menu options.
  3. Confirm to add.

For Android (Chrome):

  1. Tap the three vertical dots menu in the top right.
  2. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
